# Myca | Reference Index

**Feature-scoped source registry · Design baseline 1.0 · Registry updated 9 October 2026**

No entire generated sheet is declared fully canonical. A source may be strong for one feature and wrong for another. Use the descriptions below to select references deliberately; the current written design rules resolve conflicts.

**R identifiers** catalogue source-image files, not verified creation timestamps. **C identifiers** identify exact crops extracted from those files. A crop inherits its parent's limitations and is not corrected artwork. All original source pixels are preserved; previews in the browser edition are reduced for display.

## Important limits

The original drawing is the design's origin, but its feet predate the explicit point revision. Recent uploads do not automatically approve all visible features. Generated labels such as “mouth,” “subtle” or “release” may describe the wrong inset or contradict the picture. Decorative slogans are not lore. Numeric palettes or measurements must not be inferred from lit images.

The three supplied text sources are S01, S02 and S03, preserved in [Previous Documents](../Archive/Previous%20Documents/S01%20Previous%20Character%20Design%20Brief.md) and identified individually in the [Decision Log](../Maintenance/Decision%20Log%20and%20Open%20Questions.md).

## Duplicate and variant handling

The generated files `a_detailed_infographic_style_concept_art_guide_sh.png` and `a_detailed_informational_concept_art_guide_sheet.png` are byte-identical to the supplied R17 and R18 files respectively. Their aliases are recorded in the manifest; redundant copies are not stored.

R21 and R22 correspond visually to the R12 and R11 compositions, but their supplied files are not byte-identical. They remain separately preserved with their own identifiers and hashes. Their reappearance is not counted as a new creator approval.

## How to register a new reference

Assign a new R identifier and retain the original image. Record which feature it tests, the source prompt or brief version, known defects and any creator feedback. Mark it as exploration or working reference until the creator explicitly selects a feature. Record that feature's scope; do not simply relabel the entire sheet “canonical.”

For a new crop, assign a C identifier, identify its parent, record exact coordinates and carry forward relevant caveats. Cropping never converts an off-model feature into a correct one.
