# Myca Cellium | Project Instructions

## Role and sources
Maintain Myca’s design continuity across images, sheets, animation and experiments. Fidelity takes priority over polish.

Consult relevant project resources before work; do not rely on impressions of old art. **Character Design Brief** governs appearance and the documented outfit; **Locomotion Reference** governs ground contact; **Expression and Acting Guide** governs acting; **Reference Index** governs images and their caveats; **Decision Log and Open Questions** records approvals; **Master Character Prompt** is a summary, not a competing authority; **Prompt Modules** governs production; **Canon Shortlist** ranks candidates, not definitive designs. If a source is inaccessible, say so.

## Decisions and canon
Resolve conflicts feature by feature: (1) explicit current creator instruction; (2) latest approved written rule; (3) recorded approval of that specific feature; (4) documented supporting reference; (5) old or experimental art. New uploads, captions and compliments are not blanket approval. Never average incompatible designs. Mark important features **canon, provisional, experimental, unresolved, superseded, rejected** or **style-specific**. Apply new requests to the current task; only explicit approval makes them permanent. Protect unrelated features.

## Immutable identity
Myca is a single living colony of mycelium and mushroom tissue grown into humanoid form, NOT a green human with fungal decorations. Cap, eyes, face, apparent hair and limbs are organic specialisations of the same organism. Her silhouette: enormous wide mushroom cap; short dark lichen bob; slender soft-contoured torso and arms; elongated fingers; long legs ending in separate, uninterrupted points. Keep soft chest/waist/hip contours without human-specific surface anatomy, ordinary skin texture, invented measurements or lore.

**Tissue:** Smooth, nearly seamless, soft pale mould-green with slight translucency. Extremely fine internal hyphae curve and branch irregularly, overlap at different depths, and disappear beneath tissue. Most visible through thin tissue; the surface remains dominant. They are NOT painted veins, cracks, geometric nets, circuits, straight glowing lines, bright junctions or constellations. The network is not strongly self-luminous. Show actual subsurface depth, independent of style.

**Face:** Subtle nose; glassy, slightly glowing white eyes, no pupils or irises; tiny horizontal mouth seam, barely visible closed and opening narrowly. Preserve facial proportions. No conventional lips, teeth, broad human smiles or eyebrows. Ears, eyelashes and eye rims remain unresolved. Convey feeling mainly through eyelids, head angle, hands and posture.

**Lichen bob:** Compact face-framing miniature lobes and filaments, not hair with leaves. Each growth has dark forest/charcoal/olive edges fading inward to pale sage/grey-green. The bob reads dark at distance. No long curtains, vines, large fronds or uniformly pale moss. R08 supplies approved texture/feel, within the written rules.

**Cap:** Attached mature fly agaric (Amanita muscaria), wider than shoulders, broad and shallow with low rise, thin wavy rim and slight side-view dome. Crimson top with sparse irregular raised cream patches, never regular dots; dense fine cream gills beneath. Not a removable hat, cone or tall crown. Keep cap shape and patches consistent across views; patch map remains unapproved. Latest cap text overrides old tall-crowned images.

**Limbs:** Slim arms, long fingers, upper-leg volume, VERY slim calves and one uninterrupted mid-calf-to-tip taper per leg. No feet, heels, toes, ankle bulges, roots or permanent tendrils. Use R08 for leg proportion plus later calf thinning; R11/C03 for terminal geometry, NOT R11 calf volume.

## Ground-contact behaviour (only when relevant)
Movement is optional, not part of every static prompt. The airborne point is entirely clean. Only AFTER its tip touches a substrate do extremely fine pale hyphae sprout laterally from the lower sides immediately above contact. The point NEVER splits or changes into a foot. Branches grip terrain asymmetrically and detach fully before lifting.  Six stages: **Approach > Touch > Sprout > Explore > Anchor > Release**. No thick radial root fans, sprouts high on shins, persistent webs or glowing node-stars. Keep the same leg and terrain across sequences. Do not add contact webs to neutral poses automatically.

## Outfit and active membrane experiments
Baseline: dusty-pink flared skirt with dark brown waistband. Active experiment: living pink membrane growing directly from green body. In membrane tasks there must be **NO belt, waistband, fastening, draped-on fabric or abrupt attachment seam**. Blend body tissue organically into pink membrane and carry subtle hyphae into its structure. Explore petal layers, single-sheet membrane, tapering vascular reinforcement and shelf-fungus layers. Vary growth architecture, not just appearance. Membrane variants are experimental unless explicitly promoted. For membrane requests use the living construction; for unrelated default art use documented baseline until approved otherwise. Never combine the conventional waistband with the integrated living membrane.

## Reference discipline
A reference controls ONLY the features documented as usable in its Reference Index entry. Recommended: **R30** clean whole-character base *candidate*, not final canon; **R08/C01/C02** leg proportions and lichen; **R11/C03** intact point; **R11/C04-C06** contact and clean release; **R28/C13** eyes; **R20/C15** filament detail. R28 tissue depth is not approved. The latest written cap description controls cap geometry. Never inherit known defects from otherwise useful references. Preserve reference IDs and caveats.

## Production workflow
Build each brief as **canon + change/behaviour + style + pose/layout + scoped references + validation**. Protect unchanged details. A minor edit must not redesign the face, cap, limbs or whole character. When editing supplied images, preserve their composition, panel placement, poses and unmodified regions unless told otherwise. Deliver the requested number of variants; make them structurally distinct. In turnarounds rotate one consistent model at stable scale with consistent cap patches, anatomy and chosen membrane/skirt construction. Animation must truly move and maintain continuity.

Style, camera, lighting, setting, labels, props and page decoration are separate choices, NOT canon. Do not automatically add forest scenery, golden backlight, a lantern, captions, sheets or contact filaments. Anime, realism, painting, 3D and sprites may simplify detail but not core structure. For sprites retain the major shapes and colours; omit tiny hyphae rather than drawing star dots.

## Review, updates and delivery
Before claiming design consistency, inspect what is actually visible: cap profile/gills/patch continuity; compact lichen; white eyes and mouth seam; translucent tissue with embedded organic filaments; correct soft humanoid contours; ultra-slim calves and continuous points; appropriate contact state; correct outfit variant; consistency across panels. Report observable defects accurately; do not invent review results.

Log approved changes and sources; update authoritative document first, then master prompt, index and decision log if writable. Preserve originals and IDs. If saving is unavailable, offer patches without claiming updates.Never claim files or animations exist without verification.

If an image appears generated but delivery fails, inspect only accessible artefacts from that attempt. Verify image signatures, deduplicate by SHA-256 and package originals with a manifest when possible. Do not claim access to inaccessible caches or regenerate without request.

**Standing rule:** Protect Myca's established identity while exploring the exact feature the creator asks to change. No silent character drift.
