# Myca | Definitive Canon Sheet Shortlist

**Ranked review 0.1 · 9 October 2026 · Recommendation pending creator selection**

## Recommendation

Use **R30 as the cleanest base candidate**, constrained by **R08 for the previously selected leg proportions and lichen texture** and **R11 for the intact pointed termination**. Keep **R28** as the most useful consolidated-overview candidate to repair, and **R20** as a tightly cropped material donor.

This is an ordered shortlist for assembling a definitive reference set, not a declaration that any complete generated sheet is already definitive. A higher overall rank cannot overrule a more specific creator-approved feature on a lower-ranked sheet. The physical design baseline, master prompt, locomotion specification and earlier approvals remain unchanged.

## How candidate strength was judged

The order is an art-direction assessment of usefulness for the next definitive sheet. It considers conformity to settled design rules, explicit creator selection, readable geometry, consistency within the image, the amount of stale information that must be removed, and the distinct contribution the candidate makes to the set. Rendering polish, recency, busy layouts and captions claiming correctness do not earn priority. Detail omitted by a simplified rendering is not automatically a contradiction; however, a simple sheet cannot settle a material feature it does not show.

The assessment uses the live resource's feature-scoped index and design brief, the visible discussion and inspected candidate pixels. R27 and R28 retain their reserved identifiers from the earlier downloadable bundle and are registered here from their actual original snapshots. Other registered identifiers are unchanged, including R08 for selected proportions and R20 for the material interpretation.

## Ordered five-candidate shortlist

### 1. R30 | Soft-shaded three-pose sheet

**Role:** Lead whole-character base candidate.

**Evidence and approval scope:** Creator-supplied supporting reference. This lead ranking is an editorial recommendation, not recorded creator approval.

**Why it ranks here:** The three poses keep the main colour masses, compact bob, restrained face, skirt/waistband relationship and clean pointed endings easy to inspect. Compared with R29, fewer competing effects and no lower-leg bands make it a cleaner starting point.

**Retain:** The readable overall character, compact head/hair mass, quiet face, and the lantern / pointing / seated gesture family.

**Required corrections / do not inherit:** Reduce calf fullness and restore the long narrow taper using R08 plus R11. Resolve the broad shallow cap as one object, restore small lichen construction and demonstrate internal tissue depth at detail scale. Do not infer fixed measurements from the poses.

**Reference boundary:** Use as the base candidate to refine, not as authority over R08's selected proportions or R11's point geometry.

## Recommended reference stack

For a **new static definitive sheet**, start with R30's readable whole-character base. Constrain the legs and lichen using R08's selected features, apply the later calf thinning, and use R11/C03 to keep each termination intact. Add R28/C13 for eye volume and R20/C15 for fine irregular filament detail only when those details are being worked on.

For **locomotion**, use the separate behaviour page plus R11/C04-C06 and optional R18/C08. A clean airborne point remains mandatory. Do not import that sequence into the condensed master character prompt or add contact webs to every neutral pose.

Do not submit all five complete pages as an undifferentiated image-reference stack. The shortlist crops exclude neighboring captions and unrelated panels; they do not correct geometry, material or colour inside the retained artwork. R28/C14 is explicitly comparison-only and should not be treated as an approved material target.

## What the final sheet still needs to settle

**Cap:** One broad, shallow, mature fly-agaric volume with a consistent rim and patch layout across genuinely distinct angles. The latest written cap description remains the authority; there is no approved all-angle cap model in this shortlist.

**Legs:** The selected long proportions, reduced calf fullness and single continuous termination must coexist in the same figure, not in separate contradictory detail panels.

**Internal tissue:** Fine irregular strands should be visibly embedded under the smooth outer material, with partial concealment and different apparent depths. A brighter line network or a caption saying "not painted on" does not settle this. None of these candidates is being certified as the final material solution.

**Face and acting:** Stable white eyes, tiny seam, restrained expression and no silently adopted human-specific marks. Ear details, exact eye rims and related unresolved construction stay open.

These are review targets drawn from existing decisions, not new body measurements, an approved patch map, shader values, timing parameters or lore.
