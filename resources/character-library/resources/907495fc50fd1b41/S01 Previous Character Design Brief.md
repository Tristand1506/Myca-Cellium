# Myca --- Character Design Brief

This page tracks **standalone descriptions** of Myca's canonical design.
Each section should work independently when copied into an image prompt,
art brief, model sheet, or animation note without relying on another
reference.

## Character Concept

Myca is a gentle fungal humanoid formed entirely from living mycelium
and mushroom tissue. Her humanoid silhouette is simply the shape in
which the colony has grown. She has smooth pale mould-green fungal
tissue, subtle internal hyphae, glassy pearlescent-white eyes, a tiny
mouth seam, compact lichen-like bobbed hair, an enormous mature
fly-agaric cap, slender pointed legs, and a simple dusty-pink skirt with
a dark brown waistband. Her design is botanical, quietly alien, elegant,
and approachable.

## Body Material

Myca's exterior is smooth, soft, nearly seamless pale mould-green fungal
tissue with slight translucency. Beneath it lies an intricate but
understated network of extremely fine mycelial filaments, irregularly
branching at different depths. The network resembles real fungal
mycelium rather than veins, cracks, circuitry, or constellations, with
no bright intersection nodes. Warm backlighting produces subtle
subsurface scattering and reveals deeper layers of the colony.

## Face

Myca's face is smooth, minimal, and gently expressive, appearing
naturally grown from one continuous fungal surface. Her mouth is a tiny
horizontal seam that is nearly invisible while closed and parts only
slightly when she speaks. Her facial acting is restrained, relying on
subtle eyelid movement, head angle, posture, gesture, and body language
rather than exaggerated facial deformation.

## Eyes

Myca's eyes are smooth, glassy structures filled completely with soft
pearlescent-white bioluminescence. They contain no visible internal
markings and possess unusual depth, as though the luminous white
continues far beneath their surface. The glow is gentle and milky rather
than dazzling.

## Lichen Hair

Myca's hair preserves the silhouette of a compact short bob but is
actually specialized lichen-like fungal growth. From a distance it reads
as a dark textured hair mass; close inspection reveals many tiny
overlapping foliose and filamentous structures. Each growth is darkest
around its extremities in charcoal-green, forest-green, muted olive, and
near-black, fading inward toward pale sage and grey-green. The
structures remain small and dense so the established bob silhouette is
never replaced by large leaves, vines, or hanging fronds.

## Mushroom Cap

Myca grows an enormous living mushroom cap based specifically on a
**mature fly agaric (Amanita muscaria)**. The cap is very broad,
low-profile, and horizontally dominant, substantially wider than her
shoulders. Its mature form has a shallow central rise flowing into a
wide expanded canopy rather than a tall conical crown.

The brim projects far beyond her head and has a thin, gently wavy,
naturally irregular edge. From the front it forms a broad sweeping
silhouette with only a modest central rise; from the side it remains a
shallow dome with a long horizontal projection; from above it is broadly
circular but subtly asymmetrical.

The upper surface is deep crimson to scarlet red with a limited number
of prominent irregular cream-white patches and occasional smaller
fragments, naturally varied rather than evenly distributed. The
underside contains densely packed warm-cream radial gills. The thinner
rim is subtly translucent and can glow amber-cream when backlit.

**Canonical cap silhouette:** enormous width, shallow mature fly-agaric
dome, low central rise, expansive irregular brim, gently wavy thin edge.

## Arms and Hands

Myca's arms are slender and graceful. Her hands have elongated delicate
fingers with softly tapered tips. The thin fungal tissue of her palms
and fingers allows the internal mycelial network to become slightly more
visible, particularly under warm transmitted light.

## Legs

Myca's upper legs retain recognizable humanoid proportions. Below the
knee they become increasingly slender and spindly, with very little calf
bulk. From approximately mid-calf downward, each leg forms one smooth
uninterrupted elongated taper, continuously narrowing into a single
elegant terminal point. There is no ankle-shaped transition or separate
structure at the end. When lifted, each lower leg resembles a long
pale-green fungal spike.

Locomotion and temporary ground-contact hyphae are maintained separately
in the **Myca Locomotion Reference**.

## Clothing

Myca wears a simple soft dusty-pink skirt with a dark brown waistband.
The clothing remains visually understated so it complements rather than
competes with her fungal biology and strong mushroom-cap silhouette.

## Personality

Myca is gentle, curious, patient, observant, and quietly intelligent.
She notices tiny organisms and environmental details others overlook.
Her movement is deliberate and practiced, and she appears naturally at
home in woodland environments rather than merely visiting them.

## Palette

Pale mould-green fungal tissue; deep forest green, charcoal-green, muted
olive and pale sage lichen; crimson-to-scarlet mushroom cap; warm cream
gills and cap patches; dusty pink clothing; dark brown waistband; warm
golden woodland light.

## Design Rule

Myca should never read as a human character decorated with mushroom
motifs. She is **one sophisticated fungal colony whose body, eyes, hair,
cap, limbs, and internal mycelium are specialized parts of the same
living organism**.
