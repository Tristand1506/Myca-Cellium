# Myca --- Master Character Prompt

Using the attached Myca artwork as the primary visual reference, create
a detailed PG fantasy illustration of **Myca**, a gentle fungal
humanoid. Preserve her established silhouette, proportions, clothing,
personality, enormous mushroom cap, lichen hair, facial simplicity, and
overall design language rather than redesigning her.

## Core Biology

Myca is a living colony composed entirely of **mycelium and mushroom
tissue** that has naturally grown into a humanoid form. Every visible
feature should feel like a specialized expression of the same
interconnected organism.

Her exterior is smooth, soft, nearly seamless **pale mould-green fungal
tissue** with subtle translucency. Beneath it, show a restrained network
of extremely fine branching hyphae: irregular, softly curved, layered at
different depths, and partially obscured by the outer tissue.

The mycelium should resemble real fungal growth rather than veins,
cracks, circuitry, or constellations. Avoid bright nodes, evenly spaced
intersections, geometric patterns, and excessive glow. It is slightly
easier to perceive through thinner areas such as the hands, forearms,
shoulders, neck, face, and lower legs. Gentle subsurface scattering may
reveal deeper layers in warm backlight.

## Face

Her face is gentle and expressive but unusually smooth, minimal, and
distinctly fungal. Facial contours appear naturally grown from one
continuous surface.

Her **mouth is a tiny horizontal seam**, nearly invisible when closed.
When speaking, the fungal tissue gently parts along this seam into a
narrow opening before settling together again. Keep it subtle and avoid
conventional exaggerated mouth shapes.

Her eyes are smooth, glassy structures filled entirely with **soft
pearlescent-white bioluminescence**, with no visible internal markings.
They should feel unusually deep, as though the luminous white continues
beneath the surface.

Expression is restrained. Keep her fundamental facial anatomy
consistent. Communicate emotion mainly through slight eyelid changes,
minute movement of the mouth seam, head angle, posture, hands, body
lean, and cap orientation rather than exaggerated facial deformation.

## Lichen Hair

Preserve Myca's established **compact short-bob silhouette**.

Her apparent hair is specialized lichen-like fungal growth. From a
distance it reads as a dark textured bob; close inspection reveals many
small, densely overlapping foliose and filamentous structures.

Keep individual growths small and delicate so they do not alter the
overall bob silhouette. Their extremities are dark and richly colored in
forest-green, charcoal-green, muted olive, and near-black, fading inward
toward washed-out sage and grey-green around their centers and branching
veins.

Avoid large leafy locks, vines, or long hanging lichen fronds.

## Mushroom Cap --- Mature Fly Agaric

Myca's enormous mushroom cap is a living extension of the colony, based
specifically on a **mature fly agaric (Amanita muscaria)**. It grows
organically from her head rather than functioning as a separate hat.

The cap is **very broad, low-profile, and horizontally dominant**,
substantially wider than her shoulders. Its form is based on a mature
fly agaric whose cap has expanded and flattened with age rather than the
taller button or conical form of a young mushroom.

The crown has a **low, shallow central rise** flowing gradually into the
broad surrounding cap. The brim projects far beyond either side of her
head and has a thin, subtly wavy, naturally irregular edge. Some
sections may curl gently upward to reveal the gills while others settle
slightly downward.

From the front, the silhouette is wide and sweeping with only a modest
central rise. From the side, preserve a long horizontal projection and
shallow dome rather than allowing the cap to become tall or triangular.
From above, it is broadly circular but organically imperfect and subtly
asymmetrical.

The upper surface is **deep crimson to scarlet red** with natural tonal
variation. Its characteristic pale fly-agaric remnants appear as a
**limited number of prominent irregular cream-white patches with
occasional smaller fragments**, varying naturally in shape, size,
thickness, and spacing rather than forming an even polka-dot pattern.

The underside contains densely packed, fine radial gills in warm cream
to pale beige. The thinner outer rim is subtly translucent, allowing
warm backlight to reveal the gill structure and produce a soft
amber-cream glow.

The canonical silhouette is: **enormous width + shallow mature
fly-agaric dome + low central rise + expansive irregular brim + gently
wavy thin edge**.

## Arms and Hands

Her arms are slender and graceful. Her hands have elongated, delicate
fingers with softly tapered tips. Fine internal hyphae become slightly
more visible through the thinner tissue of her hands and fingers.

## Legs --- Canonical Shape

Preserve Myca's established leg proportions.

Her upper legs retain a recognizable humanoid proportion. Below the
knee, they become increasingly **slender and spindly with very little
calf bulk**. From approximately mid-calf downward, each leg forms a
smooth, uninterrupted elongated taper.

The contours continuously converge into **one elegant terminal point**.
There is no ankle-shaped transition or separate structure at the end.
When lifted, the lower leg reads as one continuous pale-green fungal
spike.

The thin calves and exceptionally long pointed taper are defining
elements of her silhouette.

**Keep locomotion and ground-contact hyphae out of the general character
design unless a separate locomotion reference is supplied.**

## Clothing

Preserve her established **soft dusty-pink skirt and dark brown
waistband**, with their simple recognizable proportions. Keep the
presentation PG and focused on fantasy creature design, fungal biology,
personality, and atmosphere.

## Personality and Acting

Myca is gentle, curious, patient, observant, and quietly intelligent.
She notices small organisms and details others overlook.

Her poses can show her carrying a lantern, studying a mushroom,
crouching beside unfamiliar organisms, carefully touching plants,
examining something between her fingers, or quietly observing her
surroundings.

Her unusual anatomy should never make her appear clumsy. She moves with
deliberate, practiced grace and feels naturally integrated into the
woodland around her.

## Visual Style

Use **detailed modern fantasy/anime character art with painterly
realism**, combining a clean appealing silhouette with convincing fungal
and botanical materials.

Use warm woodland illumination, soft volumetric sunlight, atmospheric
depth, realistic mushroom gills, delicate translucency, subtle
subsurface scattering, restrained bioluminescence, and exceptionally
fine organic detail.

Keep the palette centered around pale mould-green, deep forest green,
charcoal-green, muted olive, pale sage, crimson red, cream, dusty pink,
dark brown, and warm golden woodland light.

Biological detail should reward close inspection without making the
design visually noisy.

Myca should feel peaceful and approachable but unmistakably unusual:
**not a person decorated with fungal motifs, but a sophisticated
mycelial colony that has naturally grown into a gentle humanoid form.**

## Core Design Anchors

-   Smooth pale mould-green fungal tissue with subtle translucency.
-   Fine irregular internal mycelium, without constellation-like nodes
    or geometric branching.
-   Glassy, endless pearlescent-white eyes.
-   Tiny fungal mouth seam.
-   Restrained facial expression communicated primarily through pose and
    subtle movement.
-   Compact short bob composed of miniature lichen structures, dark at
    the extremities and faded toward their centers.
-   Enormous mature fly-agaric cap: broad, shallow, crimson, irregular
    cream patches, wavy thin brim, translucent radial gills.
-   Slender arms and elongated hands.
-   Slim upper legs transitioning into thin calves and extremely long
    uninterrupted pointed lower-leg tapers.
-   Soft dusty-pink skirt with dark brown waistband.
-   Warm enchanted woodland atmosphere.

**Tone:** gentle, botanical, curious, quietly alien, elegant,
interconnected, natural, living.
