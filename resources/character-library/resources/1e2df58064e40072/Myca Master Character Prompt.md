# Myca | Master Character Prompt

## Core prompt

Create **Myca**, a humanoid organism grown entirely from living mycelium and mushroom tissue. Preserve the following design in the requested medium and style. Her cap, lichen-like bob, eyes, face and limbs are specialised structures of the same organism, not fungal decorations on a human character.

Her outer tissue is smooth, soft-looking, nearly seamless and **pale mould-green**, with slight translucency. Beneath it, a faint network of extremely fine filaments curves, branches irregularly, overlaps and passes at different depths. Some strands are partially hidden by the outer tissue. Keep the body surface dominant. Avoid geometric meshes, cracks, bright nodes and constellation-like junctions; the internal network is not strongly self-luminous.

Her face is minimal, with a softly suggested nose and **glassy pearlescent-white eyes**, unmarked by pupils or irises. Their white interiors have depth and a gentle inherent glow. Her **mouth is a tiny horizontal seam** in the fungal tissue, barely visible when closed and parting only narrowly for speech. Preserve the same face across expressions. Use small eyelid changes, head orientation and posture rather than large smiles, visible teeth, conventional eyebrows or distorted eye shapes. Do not add human-specific surface features merely because they appear in a generated reference.

Her apparent hair has a **compact short-bob silhouette**, close to the head and jaw. It consists of tiny, densely overlapping lichen-like lobes and fine branching structures. Each growth is dark and richer at its edges and extremities, in forest-green, charcoal-green or dark olive, fading inward to washed-out sage or grey-green. It reads as a dark bob at a distance and fine lichen at close range, not long fronds or ordinary hair decorated with leaves.

Her enormous living cap is based on an **expanded mature fly agaric, Amanita muscaria**. It is much wider than her head and wider than her shoulders: broad, shallow and horizontally dominant, with a low central rise flowing into an expansive canopy. The thin rim is gently wavy and naturally irregular. Retain a shallow dome in side view, not a tall triangular crown. Its crimson-to-scarlet surface carries a limited number of prominent irregular cream-white patches and occasional smaller fragments, uneven in size and spacing. Fine cream-to-pale-beige radial gills fill the underside. The cap is organically attached, never a removable hat. Its thin edge transmits some light without requiring a particular lighting setup.

Her arms are slender, with elongated fingers and softly tapered fingertips. Her upper legs retain the established humanoid volume, then narrow below the knee through **very slim calves**. From approximately mid-calf downward, each leg is one long uninterrupted taper into a single point. No ankle-shaped transition, heel, sole, toes, forward bend, root-foot or permanent tendrils. A lifted leg ends completely cleanly. Use the selected proportion reference only for those proportions, not its superseded details.

She wears a **dusty-pink flared skirt with a dark brown waistband**. A lantern is optional, not part of her anatomy. Her behaviour is curious, patient and attentive; she uses restrained facial movement and deliberate gestures.

Preserve these physical attributes independently of rendering style, lighting and setting. Keep the presentation non-sexual and focused on character design. **Do not automatically add a sheet layout, captions, woodland scenery or ground-contact filaments.**

## Optional living-membrane outfit override

Use this only when the task explicitly requests a fungal-membrane skirt experiment. Replace the baseline skirt sentence, do not combine both constructions:

Myca's dusty-pink lower garment is a **living fungal membrane grown continuously from her pale-green body tissue**, with no belt, waistband, fastening, fabric seam or draped-on garment boundary. The body transitions organically into the pink membrane and subtle internal hyphae continue into it. Select one named architecture rather than blending candidates by default: **layered petal**, **single-sheet**, **vascular-reinforced**, **gill-frill**, **veil-streamer**, **spore-lace**, or **shelf-fungus**. Preserve all unrelated anatomy and identity features. These membrane constructions are experimental, not baseline canon.
