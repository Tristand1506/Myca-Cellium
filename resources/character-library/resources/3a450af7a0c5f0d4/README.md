## Core identity in one paragraph

Myca is a humanoid fungal colony with smooth pale mould-green tissue, subtle irregular internal filaments, unmarked pearlescent-white eyes and a tiny mouth seam. A compact bob is formed from small dark-edged, pale-centred lichen-like growth. Her enormous attached cap is a broad shallow mature fly-agaric form, crimson with irregular cream patches and cream radial gills. Slender arms and long fingers accompany upper-leg volume that narrows through very slim calves into long clean points. Her base outfit is a dusty-pink flared skirt with a dark brown waistband. Temporary contact hyphae belong to her movement system, not her permanent leg silhouette.

## Reference priority

The latest creator correction takes priority. Use an image only for the feature selected or described in its registry entry. In particular, combine **R08 for selected proportions and lichen**, **R11 for continuous-point geometry**, the **later calf-thinning instruction**, and the **latest written broad/shallow fly-agaric cap rule**. No single existing sheet is designated an entirely correct canonical model.

Superseded feet, high crowns, luminous node networks, oversized lichen, exaggerated mouths and persistent root-like endings are preserved as development history, not silently restored.

## Folder roles

**Root pages** contain the current working specification. **Production** contains task-specific implementation guidance. **References/Sheets** contains subject-specific source sheets, not unqualified approvals. **References/Details** contains eight exact crops with parent IDs and caveats. **Archive/Visual Development** retains earlier or challenged iterations. **Archive/Previous Documents** preserves the supplied text before consolidation. **Maintenance** contains the decision trail and machine-readable asset records.

## Asset accounting and limitations

The package preserves **23 distinct source-image files** from the available conversation. Two byte-identical generated aliases of the locomotion sheets are recorded without storing redundant image copies. Eight additional images are exact detail crops, not new generations, restorations or corrections. The three supplied documents are archived unchanged.

Generated captions are not reliable specifications; some source panels are mislabeled, retain known defects or contradict their own text. The physical brief and explicit creator decisions take precedence. There is no newly approved corrected turnaround, numeric measurement system, patch map or final animation rig in this release.

## Maintaining the resource

Record each proposed change, its source and the creator's approval scope in the decision log. Update the relevant authoritative page first, then the master prompt and reference index. Keep presentation choices separate. Never promote a provisional measurement, attractive render or generated caption into canon without review.
